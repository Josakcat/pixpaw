## [2.0.0] - 2026-11-09

Everything new since 1.12.0: your pet, redrawn.

### Added
- ✨ HD pets: every built-in pet is in finer pixels on the desktop, redrawn at 40 × 32: the cat in every coat, with your photo's colours and folded ears too, and every other animal. Its home, props and wardrobe match too, festival decorations aside. Pixel size → Chunky (classic) brings back the old look; the notch stays classic.
- 🐉 A three-headed dragon: dark and fierce on the desktop with a bronze edge that shows on a dark wallpaper, caramel in the notch, with a red, a gold and a turquoise horn to tell its heads apart. It flies when it goes somewhere, wears a hat on its middle head, roars three at once, plays with a roasted marshmallow and naps in a red-rock cave with a pile of gold. It breathes its speech bubbles alight.
- 🎨 Every pet comes in colours of its own, after real animals: a husky or a black lab, a scarlet macaw or an African grey, a Berkshire pig, a great white, a Komodo dragon and more, in HD and classic pixels. Pick one from the swatches on the shelf, Colour in the pet's menu or Settings; its friend gets its own. Each pet keeps its own look until you choose.
- 💬 Every pet's speech bubble comes up its own way: paw prints, a wagging bone, honey drips, feathers, stomp dust and more, settling with an edge in its colour; your own pets sparkle. Settings → Bubble effects turns them off.

### Changed
- 🗂️ The pet's menu has a Look group: Colour beside Wardrobe.
- 🔲 Pixel size, Fine (HD) or Chunky (classic), is in the pet's menu and card too.
- 🃏 A darker card: the card, its submenus and the What can I do? box stay readable over a light desktop or a window behind them (Droppy's theme).

### Fixed
- 👒 Settings, Wardrobe: the list of things to wear no longer opens and closes by itself while the page is open; its pictures follow Pixel size.
- 🃏 A long card or submenu fits the screen and scrolls.
- 🔒 The lock screen row hides only while Droppy's Agents is on.

## [1.12.0] - 2026-11-08

Everything new since 1.10.2:

### Added
- 🔍 What can I do?: type what you want in a small box over your pet and it finds it among its commands and Droppy's ("note buy milk" goes straight on its sign). Ask me in the menu, the 🔍 on the card, or Control-Option-/. Nothing that turns off, deletes or quits ever shows.
- 🃏 Your pet's card: hold on or right-click your pet for big buttons (Feed, Play, Nap, Focus, Lofi, a note), a Right now line with a button for what you'd likely do next (it never runs one itself), and the menu's groups. Pick its look: Droppy's theme, glass or solid. ⋯ opens the full menu.
- 🧰 Your pet runs Droppy's own tools: Notes, Tasks, Clipboard, the Pomodoro, joining a meeting and the scrolling capture go through Droppy, and the pet's own reminders step aside while Droppy shows them.
- 💡 A light beside the notch while something runs: blue for a long command or CI, amber when an agent needs you, mint when it's done, red for a snag. Beside the notch has Off, Moments and Always, and Droppy's own activities go first.
- 🏠 The home comes out for naps and folds away after. Your pet also goes home by itself in a call and late at night. Settings → Home.
- 👓 A bigger Wardrobe: a flower crown, a beanie, an acorn cap, glasses and sunglasses, and things you earn working together: a headset, a pencil, a tie, a name badge and a sprout.
- 🐷 A pig, with an oink, a watermelon and a mud wallow.
- 📸 Carry screenshots to Droppy's shelf, off until you switch it on in Settings → Capture.

### Changed
- 🗂️ The menu and Settings, regrouped: Right now, Ask me, Care, Work, Meetings, Home, Capture and Wardrobe. Home and Beside the notch have Settings pages of their own.

### Fixed
- 🔗 Links from other apps no longer start a page capture, and a focus session started by a link doesn't start lofi.
- 💬 Long answers to an agent's question are no longer cut short.
- 🎩 Hats sit right on a pet you drew. Thanks to trish for the report!

## [1.10.0] - 2026-11-05

### Added
- Walks on DockDoor Pro's Dock. With DockDoor Pro (ejbills' replacement for the macOS Dock) running, your pet stands on top of its bar along the bottom of the screen and walks on the icons, in front of it instead of hidden behind it. It finds the bar with the Accessibility permission Pixpaw already has; with the Dock hidden, on a side of the screen, or DockDoor Pro quit, it walks the bottom edge as before.
- Meeting notes. Choose Take meeting notes from your pet's menu (or a shortcut on Droppy's Shortcuts page, or `droppy://droplet/pixpaw/meeting`) and your pet sits by with its laptop, the tag at its side counting the minutes ("Taking notes · 12 min"). Choose what it listens to: your microphone, the Mac's sound (the call, a video or a webinar, through Screen Recording for Droppy), or both, with your side and the others' marked apart. It turns them into words on your Mac, in Thai or English, with Apple's on-device speech recognition. Choose Stop meeting notes and the model you pick sums it up: Apple Intelligence on your Mac (English, macOS 26), or Claude, Codex or Antigravity through their own command, signed in as you, with the model of your choice (Claude Haiku, Sonnet or Opus; any Antigravity model it lists; any Codex model by name). You get a summary, the decisions, the action items and the open questions, then the whole transcript ("[12:04] Others: …"), as a Markdown file on Droppy's shelf and in Documents → Pixpaw Meetings; Take meeting notes and Open the last meeting notes have a Meetings section of their own in its menu. The sound is never kept, and with Claude, Codex or Antigravity the transcript goes to that company to be summed up, the way it would if you pasted it in yourself; choose None for a transcript only. It stops by itself after three hours. Let everyone in the call know you're taking notes. Settings → Meeting notes.
- Talk to your pet. Press Control-Option-T (change it on Droppy's Shortcuts page) or choose Talk to me from its menu, and say what you'd like: "come here", "feed", "nap", "focus for 25 minutes", "stop focus", "play lofi", "take a photo", "note buy milk" (or "remind me to…"), "how much usage is left", "good kitty". It listens with its ear up until you stop talking, shows what it heard, and does it. Apple's speech recognition runs on your Mac, only while you talk to it; nothing is recorded or kept. English for now. Needs the new `microphone` and `speech-recognition` permissions; Settings → Pet → Talk to your pet.
- Touch ID to allow a risky one. When your agent asks to delete files for good, force-push, run `sudo` or touch a secrets file, allowing it from your pet's card (or the menu bar, or Option-1) now takes your fingerprint or your Mac's password, so a stray click can't let it through. Deny needs nothing. On by default; Settings → AI agents → Questions.
- Your own words on the card. A question from Claude now has a field under its answers for one of your own, like Claude's "Other"; a command it asks about has one for "no, do this instead", which goes back to Claude as the reason. Press Return to send. Comes with the pixpaw-ask hook (update your copy).
- CI after a push. When your agent pushes to GitHub, your pet waits for the Actions runs with you ("Pushed! Waiting for Build and Tests on app…"): all green and it jumps for joy; one red and it says which, a bug turns up until a push makes it green, and "See why app's CI failed" in its menu opens the run. It asks GitHub's `gh` command, signed in as you, every 20 seconds, so it's off unless you turn it on: Settings → AI agents → While they work → Wait for CI after a push. The hooks pass on the repository and branch of a `git push`.
- Gemini CLI without hooks. Your pet now follows Gemini CLI's own log too (`~/.gemini/tmp/<project>/chats`), like Claude Code's, Codex's and Antigravity's: when a turn starts and is done, the tool in use on the tag at its side, a failed command's bug and long commands.
- Your own cat, from a photo. Choose a photo of your cat and Pixpaw paints the cat in its colours: its fur, its stripes or patches, its white, and its eyes when the photo shows them. Vision cuts your cat out of the photo on your Mac; only the colours are kept, never the photo. The new coat is beside the others on the shelf. And Folded ears, for a Scottish Fold. Settings → Pet.
- AI agents, a page of its own in Settings. Everything about Claude Code, Antigravity, Codex and your other agents is now one link at the top of Pixpaw's settings, apart from your pet's own pages: your agents, while they work, on a schedule, questions, and AI Usage left with a row for each AI saying where its numbers come from.

- Claude's usage left without the status line. The Claude app doesn't run Claude Code's status line, so the menu bar and the widget's AI Usage left page had no Claude. Now your pet asks the `claude` command for it, the way Droppy's Agents widget does (a `get_usage` request: no prompt, nothing billed, started without your settings so no hooks run): soon after it starts, then every five minutes while Claude is at work. The five hours and week with when they reset, and the warnings at 10% and 3%. Settings → AI agents → AI Usage left → Claude Code: ask the claude command.
- Your agents without the hooks. Claude Code (in the terminal and the Claude app), Codex and Antigravity keep a log of each turn on this Mac; your pet now reads the end of it every two seconds, the way Droppy's own Agents widget does. So with nothing to install it says when a turn starts and is done, shows the tool in use on the tag at its side ("Editing App.tsx", "Running npm test"), waits with long commands, hunts the bug when one fails, sends helpers off and back, and for Codex knows what's left of the plan, with when each window resets, for the menu bar and the widget. Only a tool's name, a file's name, a command's first two words and percentages are kept, never what you or the agent wrote. With the hooks in they speak instead (they also bring the diff and the questions), so nothing is said twice. Settings → AI agents → Follow agents without hooks.
- AI Usage left, in the menu bar. A gauge beside Droppy's own as full as the emptiest window of your coding agents' plans, or a raised hand while one needs you. Click it for what your pet is up to, each agent's state (working, needs you, done with what it changed), and a bar for every five-hour and weekly window: Claude Code's from its status line, Antigravity's by model group (Gemini, Claude and GPT, a model with a limit of its own), Codex's and others' from their hooks. Green, amber at 20%, red at 10%, with when it resets ("resets in 1h 42m", "resets Thu 14:00") and how old the numbers are. When a question from your agent is up on your pet's card, it's at the top of the menu too, with the same answers, so you can answer from the menu bar when the card is out of sight (behind a full-screen app, or your pet off at the other end of the screen); a risky one still says why and puts no answer forward. At the pace you're going it says when a window runs dry, if that's before it resets ("resets in 41m · out in ~24m at this pace"): the five hours by the last hour, the latest minutes counting most, so getting down to work shows at once and a percent rounded down doesn't swing it; the week by your average over the last day, nights and breaks in it, so an hour of hard work doesn't make the week look gloomy; and while one plan runs low it points you to the one with the most left ("Most left: Claude 59%"). With a dev server up, Open localhost is there too. Needs the new `menu-bar` permission; off under Settings → AI agents → AI Usage left. Each AI has a section of its own, in its own color and mark (Claude Code, Antigravity with a line per model group, Codex), so it's plain whose numbers are whose; Settings has a row for each saying where its numbers come from. The same is a page of your pet's shelf widget too: the gauge button at its top turns it to AI Usage left (a raised hand while a question waits), a line per plan with both windows (paired, the emptier one), when one runs dry at this pace, which has the most left, and your agent's question with Allow and Deny, each AI's emptiest window on one line under it; the paw turns it back.
- What a turn changed. When Claude Code or Antigravity finishes in a Git repository, your pet says how much changed since the turn began, commits included: "Claude is done! 3 files · +84 −12 · 1 new file", before the plan usage left. It's on the lock screen line too. Counts only: the hooks note where `HEAD` was when the turn started and count with `git diff --shortstat`, never which files or what's in them. Comes with the hooks you already have.
- Open the app your agent started. When Claude Code or Antigravity runs a dev server (`npm run dev`, `vite`, `next dev`, `python3 -m http.server`, a port of its own), your pet watches for it to come up on this Mac, says "Your app is up at localhost:5173", and puts Open localhost:5173 at the top of its menu (your pet's or its friend's) for as long as it runs. The hook passes on only the port.
- Pixpaw on Droppy's lock screen. One line for when you come back to the Mac: an agent that needs you ("Claude needs you"), the focus or break clock counting down with its ring, a command still running ("npm test · 4 min"), an agent that hit a snag, is working or is done, and otherwise what your pet is up to ("Studying to lofi beats", "Napping in the notch"). Both of Droppy's styles are filled in: the strip, and the ring with the minutes left in it. Needs the new `lock-screen` permission.
- A warning on risky asks. When your agent asks to delete files for good (`rm -rf`, `find -delete`), force-push, delete a branch, throw away changes (`git reset --hard`, `git clean -f`), drop a table, run `sudo` or a script piped from the internet, wipe a disk, publish a package, read the keychain, or touch a `.env`, key or credentials file, your pet comes over with its hackles up and hisses (the dog barks). The card says why in red ("Careful: deletes files for good"), Allow is no longer the highlighted button, Always allow is left off, and Option-1 to Option-4 only work after two seconds, so a reflex can't allow it. A click still can.
- Your year together. Your pet keeps a tally of the year (minutes focused, Pomodoro rounds, bugs caught, games played and won, long commands waited out, helpers' parcels, notes done), counts only and on this Mac only, for a card at the end of the year. See it, or clear it, under Settings → Wardrobe and home → This year together.
- Bug hunts. When your agent's tests or build fail, a little pixel bug turns up on the floor and scuttles about, running off whenever your pet comes after it. When the same command passes again, your pet runs over, catches it and earns stars. One bug per command, three at most. Part of Long commands and helpers.
- A warning before your plan runs out. When Claude Code, Codex or Antigravity is down to 10% of its 5-hour or weekly limit, your pet comes over to say so, and once more at 3%. For Claude it reads what the status line keeps (see the README), so the warning can come mid-turn.
- Lofi through your pet. Choose Play lofi from its menu (or a shortcut, or `droppy://droplet/pixpaw/lofi`) and it puts on beats to study to from where you listen: Lofi Girl's live radio on YouTube, her playlist in Spotify (which starts by itself) or Apple Music, or a link of your own. While lofi plays your pet puts its headphones on and studies along at its laptop, nodding slowly, instead of dancing. Turn on Lofi with each focus session and starting a focus session starts it too; a Pomodoro break or the end of the session pauses it, and the next round picks it up again. "Nudge me back to work" leaves a lofi tab alone, YouTube or not. Settings → Breaks and focus.
- Long commands. When Claude Code or Antigravity runs something that takes a while (tests, a build, a deploy), your pet sits at its laptop and waits with it, the tag at its side counting the time ("npm test · 4 min"). The longer it takes, the funnier: popcorn first, then it turns an hourglass over, after ten minutes it gathers cobwebs, and after twenty-five it dozes off. When the command is done it jumps for joy in confetti, or gets a little rain cloud if it failed. Needs `PostToolUse` and `PostToolUseFailure` hooks for Claude (see the README); the Antigravity plugin does it by itself.
- Helpers. When your agent sends a helper off on a task (a Claude Code subagent, an Antigravity subagent), a little one of your pet's kind walks in, sits down at its own laptop beside it, and when it's done brings the result over in a parcel before heading home. Up to four at a time. Needs `SubagentStart` and `SubagentStop` hooks for Claude. Settings → AI agents → While they work → Long commands and helpers.
- Characters and rock, paper, scissors. Your pet and its friend each have a character of their own (competitive, loves a bet, playful, sleepy, calm or thrifty, two each). When both are sitting about they play rock, paper, scissors for make-believe coins, a hand over each head: a gambler bets big, a thrifty one small, a draw goes again, and your pet says what it won or lost. Their characters, coins and score are under Settings → Friend, with Start over for new characters.

### Changed

- Your pet's menu is tidier: Care, Play, Work and Meetings sections, with what each is at on the right (Focus "25 min", Play lofi "YouTube", Feed "Hungry", Wardrobe "185 ★"). Photo, GIF and whole-page capture are together under Capture; what can't wait comes first, together (a meeting about to start, Open localhost, a failed CI run); Talk to me leads Care; while focusing it offers Stop focus with the time left, and while lofi plays, Pause lofi. With a friend out, Play has rock, paper, scissors.
- Settings, regrouped by what you're there to do. Your pet's own card (show it, character, coat, sounds, beside the notch) comes first, then three groups: Your pet (wardrobe, friend, moving about, your own pets), Working with you (AI agents, breaks and focus, notes and countdowns, meeting notes, talk to your pet) and Around you (what it tells you, capture, festivals). Each link says what's set ("Black cat · competitive", "3 notes · 2-day streak"). Talk to your pet and Capture (photo, GIF, a whole web page, posing for screenshots) have pages of their own, and a new Permissions page shows what Droppy is allowed, with Allow… for what isn't, and when each agent hook last spoke, with how to set up one that hasn't. The (i) tips are two sentences now, and rows that are about any pet say "your pet", not "the cat".

### Fixed
- Antigravity's "3 files · +84 −12" when it's done, and Open localhost when it starts a dev server, were missing for anyone who installed the Pixpaw plugin with `agy plugin install`: the install copies only the plugin's own folder, and what counts the changes and finds the port was one folder up. It's in the plugin's folder now. Install the plugin again once (`agy plugin install /path/to/Tools/agent-hooks/antigravity`) and restart Antigravity.

- Rock, paper, scissors is easier to follow: the hands are drawn bigger and plainly different (a round fist, an open hand with tall fingers, two fingers in a V; before, all three looked much alike), and your pet says how it went ("Paper covers rock! I won! +12 coins"). A draw that went again could leave both pets stuck playing; now the game ends cleanly.
- Rock, paper, scissors from the menu did nothing once one of the pets had lost all its coins, until the next day's pocket money. Now the other one spots it 20 coins ("Here, 20 coins for my friend. Let's play!") and the game goes ahead. If neither can spare any, or the friend is busy, your pet says so instead of doing nothing, and the friend joins from a stroll as well as from sitting. A game you ask for also starts sooner: your pet runs over instead of strolling (crossing a wide screen took half a minute), and starts at once when it's already beside its friend.
- The second pet's menu works like your pet's: Rock, paper, scissors (your pet comes over to play), photo, GIF, capture, focus, lofi and a note did nothing from it before.
- The second pet's Wardrobe: it dresses with your pet's stars (every star it earns goes to your pet), so everything your pet has unlocked is there for it too, and what it wears is kept for next time. Before, it counted stars of its own, which stay at 0, and everything was locked.
- Antigravity's plan left counts a model's own limit too: with Gemini 4 Argon, which has a weekly limit of its own beside the Gemini one, your pet says the lower of the two ("week 96% left", not 100%).
- The shelf widget's line under your pet's name shows its bell, timer, cup or sparkles again, instead of a line of code ("Image(provider: …)") while it's quiet, focusing, on a break or summing up your agent's work.

## [1.9.0] - 2026-10-26

Google Antigravity joins Claude Code, a tag that says what your agent is working on, answers from the keyboard, GIFs of your pet to share, and scheduled runs that bring their results.

### Added

- Antigravity. Google's Antigravity (the app, the IDE and the `agy` CLI) now reaches your pet like Claude Code does: it says when Antigravity starts, finishes (with how much of your plan is left, "5h 93% · week 96% left") or fails, keeps the Mac awake while it works if you want, shows what it's working on on the tag at its side ("Editing App.tsx", "Running npm test"), and brings your Automations' results when they run on schedule, kept on its sign. Install the Pixpaw plugin once with `agy plugin install /path/to/Tools/agent-hooks/antigravity` (see the README). Answering Antigravity from the pet's card isn't there yet.
- Record a GIF of your pet. Choose "Record a GIF" from its menu and it films five seconds of whatever it's doing (walking, napping, dancing, coding along) as a looping GIF with big crisp pixels, saved where your screenshots go; your pet brings it to the shelf, ready to drop into a chat. Drawn from its own art, so it needs no permission. Also Settings → What it tells you, a Record a GIF of your pet shortcut, and `droppy://droplet/pixpaw/gif`.
- Answer with the keyboard. While Claude's question is up on your pet's card, Option-1 to Option-4 pick an answer and Option-0 hands it back to Claude, from any app. The keys are only held while a card is up; the rest of the time they're yours.
- See what Claude is working on. While Claude Code works, a little tag at your pet's side says what it's doing right now: "Editing CatController.swift", "Running swift build", "Reading README.md", "Searching the web". Only a file's name and a command's first words, never a path or the rest of a command, and it's hidden while your pet is quiet. Needs a `PreToolUse` → `pixpaw-agent.sh Claude tool` hook (see the README). Settings → What it tells you → What it's working on.
- Scheduled runs. ## [1.4.0] - 2026-10-22
